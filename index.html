    I’m presenting preliminary results from ShepBERT at the University of Hawaiʻi Cancer Center × Google Cloud AI Research Workshop 2026. ShepBERT is a BERT-style clinical language model pre-trained on 230,000+ patient EHR records and designed to predict 1-year cancer risk, with a focus on Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander populations, who face a disproportionately high cancer burden relative to the mainland US. View the poster (PDF).
