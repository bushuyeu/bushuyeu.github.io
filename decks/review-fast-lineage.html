Where FAST came from, and what it led to

The paper

FAST in one slide

The problem

RT-2 and OpenVLA turn each action dimension at each time step into one of 256 bin tokens. At high control rates neighbouring tokens are almost the same, so the model learns to copy the last token.

The fix

Compress each one-second action chunk before tokenizing: a discrete cosine transform per dimension, scale and round as JPEG does, then byte-pair encoding.

How prior work shaped FAST

OpenVLA and RT-2, then π0

Binning, kept and replaced

  • FAST keeps their recipe: a VLM fine-tuned to write action tokens, trained with next-token prediction.
  • It changes only the tokenizer. Its baseline is labelled “OpenVLA-style” (Fig. 2).
  • Karl Pertsch, FAST's first author, co-wrote RT-2, OpenVLA, Octo and DROID: FAST fixes a limit of his own earlier models.

π0: same lab, the base model

π0 is PaliGemma-3B with a flow-matching action expert. π0-FAST keeps the backbone, drops the expert and writes FAST tokens into the VLM's vocabulary.

The paper compares the two decoders on one model: about 5× less training compute for π0-FAST, about 750 ms against 100 ms per chunk at inference.

How later work extends FAST

Knowledge Insulation does FAST's future work

“…testing the combination of compression-based action encodings with non-autoregressive decoding approaches like diffusion…”FAST, discussion of future work
  • Knowledge Insulation (May 2025; Driess, Pertsch, Levine and others): the VLM learns from FAST tokens, a flow-matching expert produces the actions, and a stop-gradient keeps the expert from damaging the VLM.
  • Train fast like π0-FAST, run fast like π0. π0.5 and π*0.6 use the same recipe.

Comparison

What the comparison shows

  • As a tokenizer, FAST holds up. With only the tokenizer swapped it beats binning and VQ tokenizers (ActionCodec); BEAST only matches it on LIBERO-Long.
  • Decoding is the common objection. Sequences that do not decode (OAT, ActionCodec), the delay per chunk (OpenVLA-OFT), variable length against parallel decoding (BEAST).
  • It needs robot pre-training. Without it π0-FAST drops from 86.0% to 71.8% on LIBERO (VLA-0) and learns late (BEAST).
  • Its own lab answered the speed objection differently. π0.5, Knowledge Insulation and π*0.6 train on FAST tokens and decode with flow matching.
