    Fiatlux: A Long-Horizon Benchmark for Humanoid Ladder Climbing and Light-Bulb Replacement
    Pavel Bushuyeu, Yujin Chen, Anton Nikolaev, Brian Shu, Igor Molybog
    A light-bulb replacement benchmark built on NVIDIA Isaac Lab. In one long-horizon episode a Unitree G1 humanoid positions a step ladder under a fixture, climbs it, exchanges a spent bulb for a fresh one, and leaves the spent one in a disposal crate. Twelve subtask environments are scored on difficulty-weighted gates, with reference baselines spanning RSL-RL PPO, zero-shot GR00T N1.7 vision-language-action models, and whole-body controllers.
