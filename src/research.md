## Areas

<div class="entry">
  <div class="entry__head">
    <h3 class="entry__title">Vision-language-action models for humanoid robots</h3>
    <span class="entry__meta">2025 – present</span>
  </div>
  <p class="entry__sub"><a href="https://igormolybog.github.io">Prof. Igor Molybog’s lab</a>, UH Mānoa</p>
  <div class="entry__body">
    <p>VLAs for humanoid locomotion and manipulation: how a single model can
    perceive, reason, and act across whole-body control and dexterous tasks, and
    what limits transfer between the two.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="entry">
  <div class="entry__head">
    <h3 class="entry__title">ShepBERT — clinical foundation models for Hawaiʻi</h3>
    <span class="entry__meta">2026 – present</span>
  </div>
  <p class="entry__sub">UH Cancer Center</p>
  <div class="entry__body">
    <p>A BERT-style clinical language model pre-trained on more than 230,000
    patient EHR records to predict one-year cancer risk. The work centres Native
    Hawaiian and Pacific Islander populations, who carry a disproportionately
    high cancer burden relative to the mainland US and are correspondingly
    under-represented in the data most clinical models are trained on.</p>
    <p class="muted">Preliminary results presented at the UH Cancer Center ×
    Google Cloud AI Research Workshop 2026.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="entry">
  <div class="entry__head">
    <h3 class="entry__title">Data curation for multimodal reasoning</h3>
    <span class="entry__meta">2025 – present</span>
  </div>
  <p class="entry__sub">DCVLR challenge, NeurIPS 2025</p>
  <div class="entry__body">
    <p>How to balance difficulty against diversity when sampling supervised
    datasets for multimodal models — which examples actually buy you reasoning
    ability, and which only buy you a benchmark number. Our approach took first
    place at the <a href="https://dcvlr-neurips.github.io">Data Curation for
    Vision Language Reasoning</a> challenge.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="entry">
  <div class="entry__head">
    <h3 class="entry__title">MATH-DT — sparse sensing in nuclear digital twins</h3>
    <span class="entry__meta">2025 – present</span>
  </div>
  <p class="entry__sub"><a href="https://www.aklishin.science">Prof. Andrei Klishin’s lab</a>, UH Mānoa</p>
  <div class="entry__body">
    <p>Collaborative research on dynamical models and statistical guarantees for
    sparse sensing — recovering the state of a physical system from far fewer
    measurements than it would seem to require, with bounds on when that works.</p>
  </div>
</div>
