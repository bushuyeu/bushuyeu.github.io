Privacy policy

Personal-os is a personal tool. Pavel Bushuyeu built it for himself and uses it only with his own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

With your consent, it reads and writes Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks, Contacts) so that the account owner can work with that data from software running on their own computer.

OAuth tokens and any data retrieved are stored only on the account owner’s computer. Nothing is sent to Pavel Bushuyeu or to any third party, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising. The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens.
