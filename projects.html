  2. Workshop
    robotica: video-to-robot teleoperation for the Unitree G1
    A pipeline that turns an ordinary video into motion on a Unitree G1 humanoid: PromptHMR estimates 3D pose, GMR retargets the joints, and GR00T whole-body control drives simulation or hardware. The codebase for the ʻIolani workshop on applied ML and humanoid robots.
    github.com/bushuyeu/iolani-g1-workshop
  3. Experiments
    SmolVLA force-torque experiments
    Three experiments on adding 6-axis force-torque sensing to SmolVLA, a 0.45B vision-language-action model, for contact-rich manipulation tasks. SmolVLA takes vision and joint positions as input but has no sense of touch; the experiments test whether force augmentation works at that scale.
    github.com/bushuyeu/smolvla-force-torquecheckpoints on Hugging Face
